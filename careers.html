Academic & Administrative Opportunities

Careers at Admin Business School

Join our distinguished faculty community in Bengaluru. We invite forward-thinking scholars, experienced management practitioners, and dedicated administrative professionals to help shape future global business leaders.

Invitation of Applications (Throughout the Year)

Admin Business School invites applications for Professor, Associate Professor, and Assistant Professor positions across key disciplines of management education:

Marketing Finance & Accounting Business Analytics International Business Operations & Supply Chain Management HRM & Organizational Behaviour Quantitative Techniques Retail Management Health Care Management

Teaching Positions & Eligibility Criteria

Salary: As per AICTE norms

Professor

Minimum 10 Years Experience

Essential Qualifications: Ph.D. with a First Class or equivalent MBA (PGDM as declared equivalent by AIU / accredited by the AICTE/UGC) degree with good academic record throughout and a minimum of 10 years in teaching / research / industrial experience of which at least 5 years should be at the level of Associate Professor.

Research & Consultancy: Proven experience in handling funded research and consultancy projects. Minimum of 10 research publications in high-quality indexed journals (ABDC / WoS / Scopus).

Associate Professor

Minimum 7 Years Experience

Essential Qualifications: Ph.D. with a First Class or equivalent MBA (PGDM as declared equivalent by AIU / accredited by the AICTE/UGC) degree with good academic record throughout and a minimum of 7 years in teaching / research / industrial experience of which at least 5 years should be at the level of Asst. Professor.

Research & Consultancy: Proven experience in handling research and consultancy projects. Minimum of 7 research publications in high-quality indexed journals (ABDC / WoS / Scopus).

Assistant Professor

Minimum 5 Years Experience

Essential Qualifications: Ph.D. with a First Class or equivalent MBA (PGDM as declared equivalent by AIU / accredited by the AICTE/UGC) degree with good academic record throughout and a minimum of 5 years in teaching / research / industrial experience.

Research & Consultancy: Experience in handling research and consultancy projects. Minimum of 5 research publications in high-quality indexed journals (ABDC / WoS / Scopus).

Non-Teaching & Administrative Openings

Manager - International Operations

Education: Master’s Degree in Management / Social Sciences / Public Administration / International Relations.

Experience: 10 years managing student/faculty exchange programs, international institutional collaborations, or trade associations.

Desirable: Foreign Graduate from top university, high fluency in English/French/German.

Research Associates

Education: Master’s degree in Management (HRM / Marketing / Finance) / Economics / Statistics.

Experience: 2-3 years as Research Associate in reputed research organisations along with proficiency in SPSS or statistical software.

Librarian

Education: Master’s degree in Library Science with minimum 5 years experience as Librarian.

Desirable: UGC-NET / SLET / JRF qualification.

Receptionist

Education: Minimum 12th standard from a recognised board.

Skills: Formal front-office training; proficiency in Kannada, English, and Hindi languages.

Tele Callers

Education: Minimum 12th standard from a recognised board.

Experience: Experience in educational institutes; proficiency in Kannada, English, Hindi, Tamil, or Telugu.
